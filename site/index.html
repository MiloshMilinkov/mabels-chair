What a visit feels like

An hour here should feel like a break, not an errand. A few things we always do:

  • Tea, coffee or something fizzy as soon as you sit down
  • A ten-minute head massage with every wash
  • Free fringe tidy-ups between visits, no booking needed
  • A booster seat, cartoons and a lollipop for small clients
  • Dogs welcome, as long as they're fine with hairdryers
